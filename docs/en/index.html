Process

From need to launch

Each stage has a goal and a review with you. We agree on scope, schedule and deliverables before starting.

01

Understand and scope

We review the problem, your tools and priorities. From there, we propose a solution, scope and budget.

02

Build and review

We design and develop in stages. You review progress at an agreed cadence, and we adjust priorities together when needed.

03

Deliver and support

We prepare the solution for launch and use. Together, we define the documentation, training and support the project needs.

Collaboration

A team connected to yours

Collaboration needs planning too: we agree on who participates, where we review work and how decisions are made.

Responsibilities

You know who to contact

Visibility

Work you can follow

We share progress, questions and decisions through agreed channels. You can follow the work and add context as the solution takes shape.

Decisions
